## Abstract
This preprint identifies optimal materials for laboratory realization of TET–CVTL topological saturation:  
- Graphene/hBN for room-temperature ultraclean turbulence (Re → ∞)  
- Superfluid ⁴He for dissipationless vortex lattices and de Sitter analogs  
- CVD Diamond for extreme structural containment  

Includes material comparison, experimental proposals (laser-plasma p-¹¹B, vortex manipulation), and future directions toward eternal anyon braiding and vacuum torque devices.
